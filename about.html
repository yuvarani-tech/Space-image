What is SPACE?

A life design studio

SPACE is a life design studio built for the parts of life that don't come with instructions — understanding yourself, navigating relationships, choosing a path, raising a family, and continuing to grow long after school ends.

We bring together one-to-one experiences, group sessions, workshops, assessments, and community experiences — all designed around one idea: better questions often lead to better lives.

Why SPACE Exists

Because life doesn't come with a manual

We're taught mathematics, science, and technology — but rarely taught how to understand our emotions, communicate with the people we love, make difficult decisions, or navigate the ordinary confusion of being human.

SPACE exists to fill that gap. Not by pretending to have all the answers, but by creating a space where better questions can be asked — and where people feel safe enough to actually sit with them.

Vision

A world that takes the human experience seriously

We envision a world where self-understanding, emotional intelligence, and human connection are valued as essential life skills — taught and nurtured with the same seriousness as academic or professional skills.

Mission

To help people navigate life with intention

Our mission is to give students, young adults, parents, couples, professionals, and organizations the tools, experiences, and support they need to understand themselves and others — and to move through life's transitions with more clarity and less confusion.

Foundation

Built on trust, not transactions

SPACE is built on a foundation of trust, respect, integrity, inclusivity, confidentiality, and growth. Every experience we design is rooted in genuine care for the person in front of us — not a one-size-fits-all formula.

The SPACE Framework

Think of it as a GPS for Human Growth

The SPACE Framework is the foundation of everything we do — a simple, five-part lens for understanding where you are in life, and where you're headed.

S

Self Awareness

"Where am I?"

P

People Awareness

"Who's travelling with me?"

A

Adapt

"What happens when the route changes?"

C

Career & Purpose

"Where am I headed?"

E

Evolve

"Who am I becoming?"

Our Approach

Real conversations, not rehearsed scripts

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all advice. Every person, family, and organization we work with brings a different history, context, and set of questions — so our approach is built around listening first, and designing second.

  • Person-first, not problem-first
  • Curious, not prescriptive
  • Evidence-informed, but never clinical-feeling
  • Confidential, safe, and judgment-free

The Future We Imagine

Life skills, taught like core subjects

We imagine a future where understanding emotions, communicating well, making decisions, and navigating life are taught alongside mathematics, science, and technology — not treated as an afterthought.

That's the future we're building, one school, one organization, one community, and one conversation at a time.

A Final Thought

You're a work in progress. That's good news

SPACE isn't about arriving at some finished, perfect version of yourself. It's about staying curious enough to keep updating your understanding of who you are — one honest question at a time.
